Where to Stay

There is no conference hotel block for the AI and History Conference 2026. Below are hotels near JHU's Homewood Campus across a range of price points — book directly with the hotel whenever works for you.

No negotiated conference rate. None of the hotels below have a confirmed discount tied specifically to this conference. It's worth asking directly for a "corporate" or "academic/university visitor" rate when you call or book — several hotels near Homewood extend one informally to JHU visitors. Prices below are recent listing snapshots, not guaranteed rates; confirm current pricing before booking, especially closer to October.

The Study at Johns Hopkins

3215 N Charles St, Baltimore, MD 21218  ·  ~6 min walk to Homewood Campus
The only hotel actually on JHU's Homewood Campus — boutique, upscale, and about as close as you can get. Good option if you don't want to think about transportation at all.
~$239–249+/night Book directly →

Inn at The Colonnade Baltimore (DoubleTree by Hilton)

4 W University Parkway, Baltimore, MD 21218  ·  (410) 235-5400  ·  short walk, directly across from campus and Homewood Field
Long-standing JHU-adjacent hotel, historically recommended by the university itself for campus visitors. Mid-to-upscale.
Mid–upscale Book directly →

Delta Hotels Baltimore North (Marriott)

5100 Falls Road, Baltimore, MD  ·  just north of Homewood Campus
Straightforward mid-range business hotel, also historically on JHU's own visitor-recommendation list.

Hotel Indigo Baltimore Downtown

Mount Vernon  ·  short drive/rideshare south of campus
Boutique option in the walkable, restaurant-dense Mount Vernon neighborhood, if you'd rather stay somewhere lively than right next to campus.

Hotel Revival Baltimore

Mount Vernon  ·  short drive/rideshare south of campus
Boutique hotel with a standing promo code for JHU visitors (code BLUEJAY) — worth trying when booking, though it's a general JHU-visitor rate, not something arranged for this specific conference.

Note: Hotels marketed as being "at Johns Hopkins" sometimes refer to JHU's East Baltimore medical campus, which is a separate location roughly two miles from Homewood (where this conference is held). Double-check which campus a hotel is describing before booking.
