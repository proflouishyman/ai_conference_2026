What Happens Next

  • 1
    Confirmation email: You will receive an automated confirmation shortly at the email address you provided. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
  • 2
    Application review: The organizing committee reviews all applications on a rolling basis through the August 15 deadline.
  • 3
    Acceptance notification by September 1, 2026: You will receive an acceptance or waitlist email. Accepted participants who indicated a registration fee will receive a payment link at this time.
  • 4
    Hotel block: If you requested the JHU-rate hotel block, details will be included with your acceptance email. The block cutoff is August 1, 2026 — register early.
  • 5
    Conference packet: Closer to October, you will receive your full conference packet including session schedules, meal seating assignments (printed on your name badge), logistics details, and table conversation guides.
