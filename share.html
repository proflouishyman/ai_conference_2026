The AI and History Conference 2026 takes place October 15 and 16 at Johns Hopkins University (SNF Agora Institute) in Baltimore, in partnership with the American Historical Association. Across two days, 28 sessions look at how historians are using AI and computational methods in their research and teaching. The program is written for practitioners. Online attendance is free and open to all. Every session is streamed and recorded, so you can join live or watch later. More than 1,000 people from 37 countries have already registered. Register here: https://proflouishyman.github.io/ai_conference_2026/register.html #history #AI #DigitalHumanities
