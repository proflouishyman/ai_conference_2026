Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. This policy covers CES Agora Uploader (the "Tool"), an internal tool of the Center for Economy and Society at Johns Hopkins University (the "Center").

YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of the Tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and data that the Tool sends to or receives from YouTube is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the Tool

The Tool is used only by Center staff. It signs in to one Google account, the Center's own account, and acts only on the Center's YouTube channel. It has no public users and offers no sign-in to anyone else.

What data the Tool accesses

With the Center's permission (granted through Google's OAuth consent screen), the Tool uses the YouTube API to upload videos to the Center's channel, to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and to read back the status of those videos and the identity of the Center's channel. It does not access data about viewers, commenters, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How the data is used and stored

The Tool keeps an OAuth access token for the Center's account on a Center server, readable only by the Center's account on that machine. It also keeps a log of its own uploads (file name, checksum, video ID, title and status) so staff can check what was published. This information is used only to run the Tool. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Cookies and devices

The Tool runs on a server. It does not place, access or read cookies or similar data on the devices of anyone who watches our videos.

Revoking access

The Center can withdraw the Tool's access at any time through the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, or on request, the stored token is deleted from the server within 7 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Center for Economy and Society, 3102 Wyman Park Drive, Baltimore, MD 21218, or through the Center's website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
