CES Agora Uploader

CES Agora Uploader is an internal tool of the Center for Economy and Society at Johns Hopkins University. It publishes recorded talks from the Center's academic events, starting with the AI and History Conference 2026, to the Center's own YouTube channel.

Watch our talks on YouTube

How it works

Center staff edit each recording and review it before release. The tool then uploads the finished video to the Center's channel using the YouTube API Services, and sets its title, description, thumbnail and playlist. It runs on a Center server and acts only on the Center's own channel.

Who can use it

The tool has no public sign-up and no outside users. The only account it signs in with is the Center's own Google account. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or about other YouTube users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Our Privacy Policy explains what data the tool handles, and refers to the Google Privacy Policy. See also our Terms of Service.
