Annual Margaret Levi Lecture in Political Economy: From Value Creation to Value Extraction in Silicon Valley

Tim O'Reilly (O'Reilly Media)

Friday, October 16, 2026, 4:00 to 5:00 Eastern, Main Hall

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Tim O'Reilly traces how the incentives of shareholder capitalism, from Milton Friedman's 1970 statement to what Sam Bankman-Fried called the number-go-up era, pushed Google and Amazon from organic search toward advertising and pay to play. He uses economic rents and the gap between the economies of capital and labor to explain the pattern, and asks whether the emerging artificial intelligence (AI) economy will repeat it. His remedy is mechanism design, changing the rules of the game as Unix and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) did, so that AI labs measure success by diffusion and not only by capability. Audience questions follow, including Matthew Connelly on universities, data and compute.
