Closing Remarks & Next Steps

Louis Hyman (Johns Hopkins University)

Friday, October 16, 2026, 3:00 to 3:30 Eastern, Main Hall

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Louis Hyman argues that nothing discussed at the conference was new, setting the Philadelphia Social History Project, which linked census records on punch cards, against the cliometric overreach of Time on the Cross. Quoting a 1965 review of a Yale conference, he says the barrier to early computing was the drudgery of coding and debugging, which AI now relieves. He describes teaching students to move from trusting numbers, to rejecting them as ideology, to treating them as incomplete but useful, as historians treat every document. He concludes that AI changes the scope of history but not its method, and that inspectable data pipelines restore trust in research.
