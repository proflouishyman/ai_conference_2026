Session 16 · Thursday, October 15, 2026

AI in the Archive: Live Demonstration

Loren Moulds (University of Virginia School of Law)

Thursday, October 15, 2026, 3:30 to 4:30 Eastern, Room 242

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Moulds describes a summer sprint that processed 17,500 printed Scottish Court of Session documents, about 230,000 pages, in seven weeks at a direct cost under $500, using open models on university computing. Each page was read first by Tesseract optical character recognition (OCR), which supplies page geometry, and then by a large language model (LLM) that corrects the text, followed by passes that mark document boundaries and cluster documents into cases. He sets out four commitments for keeping machine output subordinate to human description, tests the pipeline on the Bakers of Dundee case, and demonstrates a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that lets an AI assistant query the catalog. The Q&A covers attribution, the legibility of the Solr index, the roughly 10,000 pending human decisions and image formats.
