Playable Pasts: Making Classroom Games with AI

Tore Olsson (University of Tennessee), Kwok Leong Tang (Harvard University)

Friday, October 16, 2026, 11:30 to 12:30 Eastern, Room 110

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Tore Olsson does not use generative artificial intelligence (AI) and teaches with commercial games instead, building his courses Red Dead History and Grand Theft America around Red Dead Redemption 2 and the Grand Theft Auto (GTA) series. The games supply a short daily opener and the structure of a syllabus on U.S. history since 1980, framed by the neoliberal order and split into a city by day and a city by night. Kwok Leong Tang traces the shift from chatbots to coding agents that loop through tool calls, and argues that a historian can describe a game in plain English while keeping the research and the final check. He shows an Ace Attorney style game set in Qing China that took 45 minutes to build from one novel, a version built on primary documents, and a one-prompt website that yields a single HTML (HyperText Markup Language) file.
