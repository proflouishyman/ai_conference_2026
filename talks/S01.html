Opening Remarks

Louis Hyman (Johns Hopkins University), Chris Celenza (Dean of the Krieger School of Arts and Sciences, Johns Hopkins University)

Thursday, October 15, 2026, 9:00 to 9:30 Eastern, Main Hall

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Dean Chris Celenza describes testing a large language model (LLM) on a page of Beneventan script from a manuscript at Oxford, and concludes that expertise has shifted rather than ended. Louis Hyman then argues that historians' habit of source criticism and their ability to build inspectable data pipelines, including optical character recognition (OCR) pipelines for bureaucratic archives, make the discipline more relevant. Hyman closes by introducing the next speaker, Marnie Hughes-Warrington.
