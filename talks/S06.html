Teaching AI to Novices: Experiences from Gateway Computing at JHU

Mark Spindler (Johns Hopkins University, Gateway Computing)

Thursday, October 15, 2026, 11:30 to 12:30 Eastern, Room 112

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

How do you teach technical AI skills to people starting from zero, and what does it take to build a sequence of courses rather than one-off workshops? Mark Spindler, who trains teaching assistants (TAs) for the introductory programming courses in Gateway Computing at the Whiting School of Engineering, describes using large language models (LLMs) for role-play TA training and an AI-built web app for COPUS (Classroom Observation Protocol for Undergraduate STEM) observation. He argues for partnerships beyond the history department, because people with deep experience of true novices may be a better resource for humanities students starting from scratch than graduate students in computer science alone.
