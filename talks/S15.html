Network Analysis for Historians

Song Chen (Bucknell University)

Thursday, October 15, 2026, 3:30 to 4:30 Eastern, Room 112

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Song Chen shows how historians can move from sources to network graphs and metrics. Network analysis is a relational approach to data modeling, not only a visualization technique and not limited to ties between persons. He compares the kinship and marriage ties of Chinese prefects in office in the 1040s and the 1210s, and models temple cults and places in the Lower Yangtze Delta as a two-mode network built from 34 local gazetteers. In the second half he tests ChatGPT on extracting tables from a page that has not been run through optical character recognition (OCR) and on running community detection in Gephi, and he warns that the model silently set the modularity resolution to 1.0.
