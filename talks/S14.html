Session 14 · Thursday, October 15, 2026

Teaching History with AI

Nate Sleeter (George Mason University, RRCHNM), Kristin Dutcher Mann (University of Arkansas at Little Rock), Meghan McGlinn Manfra (North Carolina State University)

Thursday, October 15, 2026, 3:30 to 4:30 Eastern, Main Hall

Watch on YouTube

Abstract

Three educators discuss teaching history with artificial intelligence (AI) across the secondary and higher-ed classroom. Nate Sleeter argues that history teaching should slow thinking down and value student interpretation over a chatbot's. Kristin Dutcher Mann describes assignments that use and critique AI, including a techno-ethical audit and a comparison of large language model (LLM) answers in Spanish and English with Wikipedia and course readings. Meghan McGlinn Manfra presents a Library of Congress funded learning trajectory for historical thinking that could support personalized pathways, and the session closes with audience discussion in small groups and on Discord.
